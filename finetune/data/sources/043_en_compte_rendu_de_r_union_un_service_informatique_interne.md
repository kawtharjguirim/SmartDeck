# Internal Service IT Meeting Report

## Agenda Review
The meeting began promptly at 10:00 AM, with the primary agenda of reviewing and updating the current IT service status, as well as discussing strategic decisions for enhancing our service quality and infrastructure.

## Decisions Made

### Infrastructure Upgrade
Following a detailed analysis, the IT team decided to upgrade our network infrastructure to a more resilient model, incorporating additional redundancies to ensure a more reliable and secure network environment. This decision will be implemented in phases over the next quarter, starting with the network switches and routers. The cost of the upgrade will be approximately $150,000 and will be funded from the upcoming annual budget. 

### Employee Training
Another major decision was made regarding employee training. It was recommended that all current employees should receive a comprehensive IT security training session, to be conducted by a third-party provider, within the next quarter. The goal is to increase security awareness and awareness about the proper use of company resources. The cost of the training is projected at $20,000.

### Performance Monitoring
In terms of performance, the IT team will enhance the existing monitoring system to include more frequent checks and proactive alerts for any issues, rather than relying on scheduled checks. They have also decided to invest in a new monitoring tool that will provide more detailed insights and real-time data, which should significantly improve our ability to address performance issues swiftly. The total investment for this initiative is estimated at $40,000 and will be funded from the IT budget.

## Chiffres et Affectations

### Cost Allocation
For the infrastructure upgrade, we will allocate $50,000 from the current quarter's IT budget, and the remaining $100,000 will be planned for allocation from the next quarter's budget.
For employee training, the $20,000 is being budgeted from the current quarter's IT budget, with the remaining $18,000 projected to be covered by the upcoming quarter's budget.
For the new monitoring tool, we will allocate $40,000 from the current quarter's budget.

### Current Challenges and Priorities
Despite the challenges, the team prioritizes the improvement of the network infrastructure and employee training. The IT team is currently tracking these projects closely and is expected to report on their progress at the next scheduled meeting.

## Actions à Mener

1. The IT team is to begin planning and preparing for the network upgrade phase, including securing all necessary permissions and contracts.
2. The third-party security training provider has been contacted for the next quarter’s schedule to ensure the training sessions are well-organized and effective.
3. The new monitoring tool's procurement process will commence immediately, with the IT department in charge of finalizing the selection and purchase of the product.
4. The IT team will also begin to gather performance data, analyzing trends and patterns in the current system to provide better insights into how the upgraded system will operate.

## Conclusion
The IT team is committed to improving our services to meet the needs of our internal users. These decisions are critical steps toward achieving our goals and will be closely monitored to ensure they are effective and efficient.
