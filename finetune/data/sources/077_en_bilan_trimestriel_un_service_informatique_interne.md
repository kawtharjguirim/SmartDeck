# Quarterly Service IT Report

## Overview
This quarterly report provides an overview of the Service IT department’s performance over the last three months. The report is structured to highlight the key metrics, their current values, and their evolution from the previous quarter. This will ensure that all stakeholders have a clear understanding of the department's progress and areas for improvement.

## Metrics Overview
The Service IT department is responsible for managing and maintaining the company’s information technology infrastructure. This section will cover several critical metrics, including IT Support Incidents, System Uptime, and IT Service Usage.

## IT Support Incidents
Over the last quarter, the number of IT support incidents has decreased by 15%. In the first quarter, there were 300 incidents; by the end of the third quarter, the figure dropped to 255. This represents a reduction of 15% (300 - 255 = 45, 45 / 300 * 100 = 15%).

| Quarter       | Total Incidents |
|---------------|-----------------|
| First Quarter | 300             |
| Second Quarter| 285             |
| Third Quarter | 255             |

## System Uptime
The System Uptime has shown a significant increase of 7% in the third quarter. In the first quarter, the system had an uptime of 98.5%, while in the third quarter, it improved to 99.2%. This represents an improvement of 0.7% (99.2 - 98.5 = 0.7, 0.7 / 98.5 * 100 ≈ 0.7%).
| Quarter       | Uptime (%)         |
|---------------|---------------------|
| First Quarter | 98.5%               |
| Second Quarter| 98.8%               |
| Third Quarter | 99.2%               |

## IT Service Usage
The percentage of IT services usage increased from 75% in the first quarter to 79% in the third quarter. This growth is attributed to the company's ongoing focus on utilizing IT services to improve business efficiency.

| Quarter       | IT Service Usage (%) |
|---------------|----------------------|
| First Quarter | 75%                  |
| Second Quarter| 76%                  |
| Third Quarter | 79%                  |

## Conclusion
Overall, the Service IT department has demonstrated steady progress in reducing the number of IT support incidents, improving system uptime, and increasing the usage of IT services. Despite the significant improvements, there is still room for improvement, particularly in reducing the number of IT incidents and increasing the percentage of services usage. We plan to further enhance our processes and introduce new initiatives in the upcoming quarter to ensure sustained improvement.
