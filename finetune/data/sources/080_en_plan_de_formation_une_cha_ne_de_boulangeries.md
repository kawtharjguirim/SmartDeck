# Bakery Bakery Management Training Plan

## Overview
Bakery Bakery, a thriving chain of bakeries in the City of Lights, has recognized the importance of training to enhance the quality and consistency of their products. This comprehensive training plan aims to equip the current staff with the necessary skills and knowledge to excel in their roles and ensure the long-term success of the company. The training program will be tailored to several modules, including basic baking techniques, advanced patisserie, and quality assurance. The course duration will be four weeks, and the program will be evaluated through a series of quizzes, practical assessments, and feedback surveys.

## Modules and Participants

### Module 1: Basic Baking Techniques
- **Participants:** All Bakery Bakery staff, with a focus on kitchen helpers and inexperienced bakers.
- **Duration:** 2 weeks.
- **Content:** Introduction to baking equipment, basic baking processes, safe food handling, and basic baking techniques such as mixing, kneading, and baking.
- **Assessment:** Practical baking tasks and theoretical knowledge tests.

### Module 2: Advanced Patisserie
- **Participants:** Bakers with at least one year of experience and those interested in enhancing their skills in making pastries.
- **Duration:** 1 week.
- **Content:** Techniques for making a variety of pastries, such as croissants, cream puffs, and fruit tarts, as well as knowledge of ingredients and flavor profiles.
- **Assessment:** Practical pastry making, presentation of a pastry creation, and a flavor profile test.

### Module 3: Quality Assurance
- **Participants:** Quality assurance and quality control team members.
- **Duration:** 1 week.
- **Content:** Quality assurance policies, procedures, and regulatory requirements, including food safety management system (FSMS) audits and corrective actions.
- **Assessment:** Practical audits, corrective actions implementation, and feedback sessions.

## Duration and Evaluation

### Duration
The training program will span a total of four weeks. The training program will be conducted in an intensive four-week format, with each module running for the duration specified.

### Evaluation
The effectiveness of the training program will be evaluated through several methods. The first method will involve a series of quizzes that cover the theoretical knowledge of each module. The second method will be practical assessments where participants will be asked to prepare and serve a dish under supervision. Third, participants will fill out feedback surveys to provide insights into their experience and areas of improvement.

## Conclusion

By implementing this training plan, Bakery Bakery aims to empower its staff with the skills and knowledge to improve the quality of their baked goods, thereby enhancing the overall customer experience. The training program will be a key investment in the company's future, ensuring continued success and growth.
