# Incident Report for Pharmacies Network

## Chronology
- **January 1, 2023:** Initial alert received from one of the pharmacies regarding unauthorized access attempts to the network.
- **January 5, 2023:** Security team initiated a forensic investigation.
- **January 10, 2023:** Confirmed breach affecting the networks of 10 out of 20 pharmacies in the chain.
- **January 12, 2023:** Security patches were deployed across the network to mitigate the breach.
- **January 20, 2023:** A total of $50,000 in healthcare records were discovered to have been breached.
- **January 30, 2023:** Customer communication was initiated by the affected pharmacies.
- **February 5, 2023:** Full remediation efforts were concluded, ensuring the network was secure.

## Causes
The incident was caused by a vulnerability in the older version of a software module used by the pharmacies. This software, known as "MediSecure," had not been updated to its latest version since its deployment. An attacker exploited this vulnerability to gain unauthorized access to the network.

## Impact
The breach impacted 10 out of 20 pharmacies in the chain. The total value of healthcare records affected by the breach was $50,000. The breach also exposed the potential vulnerability of the network, which had not received timely security updates. There was no evidence of any sensitive information, such as social security numbers, being compromised, but the exposure of the network to unauthorized access posed a significant risk.

## Measures Corrective
In response to the breach, the following measures were taken:
- Security patches were immediately deployed across the network to address the vulnerability.
- A new, more secure version of MediSecure was rolled out to all pharmacies in the chain.
- Enhanced security training for all pharmacy staff was conducted to raise awareness about network security.
- A full network vulnerability scan was initiated to identify any other potential vulnerabilities, and all systems were updated.
- A dedicated incident response team was established, which includes security personnel and IT staff, to ensure a rapid response to any future incidents.

## Conclusion
The breach was a result of a vulnerability in the older version of the MediSecure software. The prompt deployment of security patches and the rollout of the new version of the software have mitigated the risk. The affected pharmacies were informed of the breach and are taking the necessary steps to ensure customer data security. The incident response team will continue to monitor the network for any further issues and is committed to improving the security of the network.
