# Weekly Meeting Report

## Meeting Overview
The Weekly Meeting was held on 15th May, 2023, at 2:00 PM. The meeting was attended by members of the Engineering Department, led by Dr. Helen Smith. The agenda for this meeting included reviewing current project progress, discussing future project plans, and discussing a recent research funding application.

## Project Progress Review
The current project, 'Project Echelon', is well underway and has seen substantial progress. A total of 50% of the initial project goals have been achieved. The team has successfully designed and tested four prototypes, of which three have been selected for further development and have received positive feedback from the market research team. The fourth prototype has been discarded due to its high cost and low performance.

## Future Project Plans
The team has identified two key future projects, Project Alpha and Project Beta. Project Alpha is expected to be the next phase of 'Project Echelon', with a budget of $100,000. Project Beta, a new initiative, has a budget of $50,000 and is expected to be completed in one month. The team is working on finalizing the scope of Project Alpha and is seeking additional funding to scale up Project Beta.

## Funding Application
The Engineering Department has submitted a research funding application for a total amount of $250,000. The proposal has been well received and has received positive feedback from the review committee. The team is optimistic that the application will be successful, which will be instrumental in achieving the goals of the department.

## Actions to be Taken
1. The team will finalize the scope of Project Alpha and submit a revised application for additional funding.
2. The market research team will begin preliminary market research for Project Beta.
3. The team will also review and update the progress report for Project Echelon, and submit it to the department.

## Future Expectations
With the successful funding of the research project, the team expects to achieve further progress in 'Project Echelon', with the introduction of new features and improvements. Furthermore, the initiation of Project Alpha and Project Beta will provide the team with new opportunities for growth and innovation. The team is committed to the successful completion of all project goals, and they are confident in the team's ability to achieve these goals.
