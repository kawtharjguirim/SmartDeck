# Meeting Report - Food Relief Initiative

## Overview

The Food Relief Initiative's latest meeting was held on January 22nd, 2024, at the headquarters of the Global Emergency Response Association (GERA). The meeting was attended by representatives from GERA, the Local Emergency Response Team (LERT), and the Data Analysis and Research Unit (DARU). The aim of the meeting was to review the progress of the food relief efforts in the recent quarter and to discuss strategies for the upcoming quarter.

## Decisions Made

### Progress Reports

- The team agreed to provide progress reports to all stakeholders by February 10th, 2024. The reports will detail the number of meals served, the amount of food received, and the number of beneficiaries. (Details on numbers and percentages will be provided by DARU)

- The team decided that the next meeting will be held in February, with representatives from other countries to be invited for international collaboration.

### Resource Allocation

- The Financial Planning and Budgeting Committee approved an additional $50,000 for the distribution of emergency rations in the upcoming quarter. This increase is to support the growing number of food-insecure populations.

### Recruitment and Training

- The HR and Training Committee recommended the hiring of 10 new staff members, with a focus on outreach and communication. These new hires will be tasked with reaching out to new communities and educating them on how to access emergency aid.

### Action Plan

- The Food Distribution Team will prioritize the delivery of emergency rations to the hardest-hit areas identified by the Emergency Response Coordination Team. This includes regions facing severe droughts, floods, and other natural disasters.

- The Procurement Team will prioritize sourcing food from local suppliers, reducing dependency on international aid. It is estimated that this strategy will result in a 20% cost reduction.

## Presented Figures

- **Number of Meals Served**: In the last quarter, the team served 1.2 million meals, up 15% from the previous quarter.
- **Amount of Food Received**: The team received 4.5 million pounds of food, up 10% from the previous quarter.
- **Percentage of Beneficiaries**: 20% of the beneficiaries were from remote and underprivileged areas, and the plan to increase this figure to 30% by the end of the year.
- **Cost**: The cost of food distribution per meal was $0.30, down 20% from the previous quarter.

## Next Steps

- The team will continue to monitor the effectiveness of the current strategies through regular feedback from beneficiaries and local authorities. The plan is to refine these strategies based on the feedback gathered.

- There will be a focus on increasing awareness campaigns to educate communities about the importance of emergency aid and how they can access it. This will be done in collaboration with the community outreach team.

- A final report will be submitted to all stakeholders by February 10th, 2024, summarizing the progress made, challenges faced, and plans for the future.
