# Annual Report

## Results of the Year
In the year 2023, the Internal IT Services department achieved significant results that positioned us as a key player within the organization. Our team successfully managed and maintained a diverse fleet of systems and servers, totaling 1500 devices. This includes over 500 servers and 1000 workstations, ensuring a robust IT infrastructure that supported our operations and contributed to the smooth running of our internal processes.

During the year, we upgraded our network infrastructure, resulting in a 50% improvement in network uptime, and a 70% reduction in network-related issues reported by end-users. The IT department worked closely with our IT Security team to implement a new security protocol which resulted in a 20% increase in our security posture, as confirmed by our latest audits.

## Our Team
Our team consisted of 45 IT professionals, including 20 engineers, 15 support technicians, and 10 project managers. Our team collaborated closely with cross-functional departments to ensure the alignment of their IT needs and objectives. We welcomed three new team members in the year, bringing our team to a total of 48, and we also conducted a comprehensive training program for all team members, aiming to enhance their skills and knowledge.

## Projects
In 2023, we undertook several projects that have significantly contributed to the success of our department. The first project involved the implementation of a new IT Security Management System (ITSMS), which was completed within our target deadline. The implementation of the new System has proven to be successful and has led to a noticeable improvement in our security posture, as mentioned previously. 

Our second major project was the upgrading of our software applications, which improved the efficiency of our internal processes. The project involved the deployment of 50 new applications and the optimization of 30 existing applications. We also achieved a 40% reduction in the time taken to deploy applications, thanks to the streamlined processes we put in place. We also saw a 15% increase in the speed of application deployment.

Our third project was the implementation of a new IT infrastructure that included the installation of a new server room and upgrading of our servers. This project has already improved our network uptime, and we expect to see a further 10% improvement by the end of the year. 

## Perspectives
Looking to the future, our IT Services department aims to continue improving the efficiency and effectiveness of our processes. We plan to expand our project management capabilities and improve our cybersecurity protocols. We also aim to further develop our software applications and upgrade our servers and infrastructure. 

In 2024, we plan to undertake several projects, including the implementation of new tools and technologies aimed at improving our IT Services. We aim to enhance our team's skillset, including the acquisition of new certifications and training programs to prepare our team for the challenges ahead. We also plan to collaborate with other departments to ensure that their IT needs are addressed and that our IT Services are aligned with their objectives. 

We are excited about the challenges and opportunities ahead and look forward to achieving even greater success in 2024 and beyond.
