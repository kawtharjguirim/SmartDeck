# E-Commerce Operations Meeting Report
## Meeting Details
The E-Commerce Operations team held a meeting on September 15, 2023, at 10:00 AM. The meeting was chaired by the CEO, Jane Doe. The key stakeholders present included the CTO, John Doe, the CFO, Maria Smith, and the Head of Marketing, David Brown.

## Decisions Made
### Product Launch Planning
The team agreed to launch a new product line in November 2023. The product line will feature eco-friendly and sustainable products, which aligns with the company's commitment to sustainability. The CEO will announce the launch on social media platforms, and the Marketing team will start the advertising campaign immediately.

### Sales Targets
The sales targets for the quarter were set at $3,500,000. The Marketing team will prioritize campaigns targeting the top 10% of customers based on their purchasing history. A $2,000,000 target has been set for the second quarter, with an additional $1,500,000 for the third quarter.

### Marketing Spend
The marketing budget for the quarter is set at $500,000. Half of the budget will be allocated to Facebook and Instagram advertisements, while the other half will be used for email marketing campaigns.

### Product Improvement
The team will review all product listings and will work on reducing shipping and stockouts by the end of the month. The goal is to maintain a stock level of 50% of the product's average monthly sales.

## Financial Report
### Sales Growth
The last quarter showed a 15% growth in total sales compared to the previous quarter. The e-commerce platform saw a 20% increase in sales from mobile devices compared to desktop devices.

### Cost Reduction
The e-commerce team managed to reduce their cost by 5% in the last quarter. The reduction in cost was mainly achieved through the optimization of delivery routes, improved inventory management, and the reduction of utility costs.

### Customer Feedback
There was a 30% increase in customer feedback received on the platform, indicating an improvement in customer satisfaction. The customer feedback is being analyzed to identify areas of improvement and new features to add to the platform.

## Action Items
