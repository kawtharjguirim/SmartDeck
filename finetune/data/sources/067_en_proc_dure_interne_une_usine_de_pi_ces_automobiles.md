# Internal Procedure Document for an Automotive Parts Factory
## Overview
This document outlines the procedures, roles, and timelines involved in the manufacturing process of automotive parts at XYZ Automotive Parts Factory. Each section specifies the responsibilities of the respective team members and the expected completion times for various stages of the process. 

## Manufacturing Process

### Stage 1: Raw Material Procurement
- Raw material procurement is managed by the Purchasing Department. They are responsible for sourcing materials from reliable suppliers. The timeline for this stage is estimated at 5 working days. The Purchasing Department ensures the materials are compliant with quality standards, and they are delivered to the Warehouse Department.
- Warehouse Department is responsible for receiving and storing all raw materials. They ensure materials are safely stored and are ready for the production process. The Warehouse Department completes this stage within 2 working days.
- Production Department oversees the entire manufacturing process. They are responsible for manufacturing the automotive parts. They begin the production process immediately after receiving the materials from the Warehouse Department. The expected completion time for the production stage is 10 working days.
- Quality Assurance Department inspects the quality of the automotive parts. They conduct inspections and make adjustments when necessary. The Quality Assurance Department completes this stage within 3 working days.
- Final Inspection Department is responsible for the final inspection of the automotive parts. They ensure all parts meet the quality standards and are ready for shipment. The Final Inspection Department completes this stage within 2 working days.
- Production Department is responsible for packing the automotive parts. They ensure all parts are packed safely and are ready for shipment. The Production Department completes this stage within 1 working day.
- Warehouse Department is responsible for dispatching the finished products. They ensure the products are dispatched within 2 working days.

### Stage 2: Production Planning
- The Production Planning Department creates the production schedule. They collaborate with the Purchasing Department, Production Department, Quality Assurance Department, and Final Inspection Department to ensure smooth production flow. The Production Planning Department completes this stage within 5 working days.
- Purchasing Department is responsible for ordering raw materials and components. They ensure that all materials are available for production. They complete this stage within 3 working days.
- Quality Assurance Department is responsible for conducting regular quality checks during production. They ensure that all produced parts meet the quality standards. They complete this stage within 2 working days.
- Production Department is responsible for maintaining production equipment and ensuring the smooth running of the production process. They complete this stage within 1 working day.
