# Market Study for a New Software Start-Up

## Market Size
The global software industry is expected to grow at a CAGR of 4.5% to reach a market size of $2.2 trillion by 2025. The market for software solutions targeted at the B2B sector is expected to be $1.5 trillion by the same year. The B2B market is forecasted to have a significant growth of 5% annually.

## Market Players
There are several established players in the software industry. The top three players in the B2B software space are Oracle with a market share of 17%, SAP with a 16%, and Microsoft with a 14%. However, the market is also witnessing the emergence of new players, including our start-up, dedicated to providing software solutions for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).

## Target Customers
Our start-up targets SMEs with a market share of 20%, which includes a significant number of businesses that need specialized software solutions to improve their operations. Our market analysis suggests that there are approximately 500,000 SMEs across the country, with a turnover of $1 million to $10 million, and 15% of these businesses have not yet adopted specialized software solutions. 

## Market Trends
There is a notable trend towards digital transformation in the SME sector, with over 70% of SMEs now investing in digital technologies to improve their operations. Additionally, the rise of mobile devices and the growth of e-commerce are also affecting the software market, with 80% of SMEs now using mobile devices and over 20% of these businesses having e-commerce platforms.

## Competitive Analysis
Our start-up has a unique value proposition, with a focus on offering customizable solutions to our clients. A survey of our potential customers indicates that 50% of SMEs are looking for customizable solutions, which is where our start-up can differentiate itself from the competition. Moreover, our start-up has the potential to offer these customized solutions at a lower cost, making it more accessible to our potential customers.
