# Incident Report
## Chronology
The incident began on January 15th, 2023, at the inception incubator, known as Seed Incubator. On this date, a software malfunction was noticed affecting the database operations. The main application that was affected was the financial management system, which was used by all incubator startups for their project budget and progress tracking. The malfunction caused transactions to fail, leading to financial discrepancies and delays. This malfunction became apparent on January 15th, but the full extent was not realized until the end of January, where significant discrepancies were reported across all projects. 

## Causes
The root cause of this incident was attributed to a coding error in the financial management software by the startup company, TechPioneers. The error caused transactions to be flagged as invalid, even when they were perfectly valid. This error occurred due to a lack of comprehensive testing and a flawed security audit before the software was launched. The developers at TechPioneers were unaware of the error, as they had only recently released the software and had not thoroughly tested it in real-time conditions.

The security audit was also a cause of concern, as the audit results indicated potential vulnerabilities but did not identify this specific error. The audit was conducted by an external security firm, SecurityGuard, which was also a new entrant in the market. The audit was conducted in a limited timeframe, with insufficient resources provided by the incubator management. 

## Impact
The financial impact of this incident was significant. By January 31st, 2023, it was estimated that the total cost to the incubator and its startups was $150,000. This cost includes: the cost of correcting the software error, which required a full audit and re-development, the loss of trust in the financial management system among the startups, and the delay in project progress. Moreover, the reputational damage caused by the incident could not be quantified, but it was expected to affect the relationship between the startups and the incubator, potentially leading to a loss of future investment opportunities.

## Corrective Measures
To address the issue, the incubator management decided to take the following corrective measures:

1. A comprehensive audit of all startups using the financial management software was conducted and confirmed no further issues with the software. This audit was conducted by an internal team of experts, which included representatives from the incubator and the affected startups.

2. A new software development contract was signed with TechPioneers, with a requirement for thorough testing and a more extensive security audit. The contract was signed on January 28th, 2023.

3. The incubator management decided to improve its audit and development processes. A workshop was organized for the startups to discuss the issue and develop strategies to prevent such incidents from occurring in the future.

4. A new set of security standards and procedures were implemented, based on the feedback from the audit and the recommendations from the workshop. These new standards were introduced on February 10th, 2023. 

5. Training on effective communication between startups and the incubator was conducted, with the objective of improving trust and minimizing the impact of future incidents.

## Conclusion
The impact of this incident was severe, and the cost and damage were significant. However, the corrective measures taken by the incubator management have brought the situation under control. The goal is to prevent future occurrences and ensure the smooth and secure operation of the incubator and its startups.
